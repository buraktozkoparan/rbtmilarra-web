MILARRA is a purpose-built music technology platform by RBT, designed to bring routing, processing and hands-on control into one focused instrument.

Built around
the performance.

MILARRA is being developed as a dedicated hardware environment for modern MIDI setups — connecting instruments and controllers across USB, Bluetooth and network MIDI while keeping musical control immediate and intentional.

RouteFlexible MIDI paths and rule-based control for complex hardware setups.
TransformMusical processing designed for live use, from chords and utilities to performance-oriented MIDI tools.
ConnectUSB MIDI, BLE MIDI and network connectivity brought together in one platform.
